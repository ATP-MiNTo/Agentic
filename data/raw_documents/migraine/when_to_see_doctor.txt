Title: When to See a Doctor for Migraine
Source: Migraine.txt
Last Updated: 2026

---

Red Flags and Warning Signs
- Sudden severe headaches should be checked.
- Headaches after a head injury need medical attention.
- Vision problems, confusion, difficulty speaking, weakness, or numbness are warning signs.
- Headaches with fever and a stiff neck should not be ignored.

When to Seek Emergency Care
- Emergency care should be sought if a headache is extremely sudden and severe, sometimes described as the worst headache of your life.
- Seek emergency help if migraines are associated with seizures, loss of consciousness, chest pain, or stroke-like symptoms such as facial drooping or difficulty moving parts of the body.

Recommended Specialist (Neurologist)
- People with frequent, severe, or unusual migraines may benefit from seeing a neurologist.
- A neurologist can help diagnose migraine types, recommend treatments, and rule out other neurological conditions.

Importance of Medical Consultation
- Medical consultation is important because migraines can significantly affect daily life.
- Migraines may sometimes be confused with other serious health conditions.
- A doctor can provide diagnosis, personalized treatment plans, and recommendations to reduce frequency and severity.

Headache Diary Recommendation
- Keeping a headache diary can help identify migraine triggers and patterns.
- Useful details include when migraines occur, symptoms, food intake, stress levels, medications used, and possible triggers.
- This information can improve migraine management and treatment decisions.
