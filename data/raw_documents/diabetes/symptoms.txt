Early Warning Signs
- Early warning signs of diabetes may include increased thirst, frequent urination, unusual hunger, fatigue, blurred vision, and unexplained weight loss.
- Some people may also experience slow-healing wounds or frequent infections.
- Recognizing these signs early can help prevent serious complications.

Common Symptoms for Type 1
- Type 1 diabetes symptoms usually develop quickly, often within a few weeks.
- Common symptoms include extreme thirst, frequent urination, sudden weight loss, fatigue, and increased hunger.
- Some patients may also experience nausea or diabetic ketoacidosis, which requires immediate medical attention.

Common Symptoms for Type 2
- Type 2 diabetes symptoms often develop slowly and may go unnoticed for years.
- Common symptoms include tiredness, blurred vision, increased thirst, frequent urination, slow-healing cuts, and numbness in the hands or feet.
- Many people discover they have Type 2 diabetes during routine health check-ups.

Symptoms in Children
- Children with diabetes may experience excessive thirst, frequent urination, bed-wetting, tiredness, irritability, and weight loss.
- Parents should seek medical attention if these symptoms appear suddenly or become severe.

When Symptoms Appear
- Symptoms of Type 1 diabetes usually appear suddenly.
- Symptoms of Type 2 diabetes develop gradually over time.
- Some individuals may not notice any symptoms until complications begin to occur.
