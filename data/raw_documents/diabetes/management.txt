Blood Sugar Monitoring
- Blood sugar monitoring is important because it helps track whether blood glucose levels are within a healthy range.
- Patients can use finger-prick glucose meters or continuous glucose monitoring devices.
- Regular monitoring helps prevent complications and allows doctors to adjust treatment plans effectively.

Insulin Therapy
- Insulin therapy is an essential treatment for some people with diabetes, especially those with Type 1 diabetes.
- Insulin helps control blood sugar levels and keeps them within a normal range.
- Insulin is available in different forms such as injections and insulin pens.
- Patients should use insulin according to their doctor’s instructions.

Oral Medications
- Oral medications for diabetes help lower blood sugar levels and improve the body’s ability to use insulin.
- Doctors prescribe different medications depending on the patient’s condition.
- Taking medications regularly and correctly is important for effective diabetes management.

Diet and Nutrition
- Healthy eating plays a major role in diabetes management.
- People with diabetes should reduce sugary foods, high-fat foods, and processed foods while increasing vegetables, fruits, and whole grains.
- Maintaining balanced meals and controlling portion sizes can help regulate blood sugar levels.

Exercise Recommendations
- Regular exercise helps control blood sugar levels and improves insulin sensitivity.
- Recommended activities include walking, jogging, cycling, and swimming.
- People with diabetes should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise each week.

Regular Check-ups
- Regular medical check-ups help monitor diabetes and prevent complications.
- Patients should routinely check blood sugar levels, blood pressure, kidney function, and eye health.
- Visiting healthcare professionals regularly allows for better treatment adjustments and overall health management.
